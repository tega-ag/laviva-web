Questions

Frequently asked questions

How do I enquire about a product?

Open the product and use the WhatsApp message in the details panel, or use the contact form. The form checks your details and prepares a message. It does not send or store that message by itself.

How do bespoke orders work?

Bespoke outfits start with a conversation. Share the occasion and any references you have. Laviva will discuss the design and quote with you. There is no booking system on this website.

How do I confirm that a product is available?

Ask Laviva on WhatsApp before you pay. The catalogue is demonstration data, so an item on the page may no longer be available.

How do I ask about delivery?

Include your area in the WhatsApp message and ask what arrangements are possible. This website does not list delivery times, shipping fees, or coverage areas.

How do I confirm a price?

Prices shown in naira are samples. Bespoke work and some hair listings say Request a Quote. The final price is the one Laviva confirms with you.
